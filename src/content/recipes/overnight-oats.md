## Ingredients

- 50g rolled oats
- 150ml milk of your choice
- 100g greek yoghurt
- 1 tbsp peanut butter
- 1 tsp honey
- 1 banana, sliced

## Method

1. Stir the oats, milk, yoghurt and honey together in a jar.
2. Cover and chill overnight, or for at least 4 hours.
3. In the morning, top with the banana and a spoonful of peanut butter.
