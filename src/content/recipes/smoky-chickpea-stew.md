## Ingredients

- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 onion, finely diced
- 3 cloves garlic, crushed
- 2 tsp smoked paprika
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- 2 x 400g tins chickpeas, drained
- 400g tinned chopped tomatoes
- 500ml vegetable stock
- 200g spinach
- Salt and pepper, to taste

## Method

1. Heat the oil in a large pan over a medium heat and soften the onion for 8 minutes. Add the garlic, paprika and cumin and cook for 1 minute.
2. Tip in the chickpeas, tomatoes and 500ml stock. Bring to the boil, then simmer for 20 minutes until thick.
3. Stir through the spinach until wilted and season well.
4. Serve with crusty bread. Leftovers keep for 3 days in the fridge and reheat at 180°C for 15 minutes.

## Notes

Cut a roasted pepper into 2cm pieces and add it with the chickpeas for extra sweetness.
