## Ingredients

- 200g spaghetti
- 40g butter
- 2 cloves garlic, thinly sliced
- 1 lemon, zest and juice
- 30g parmesan, finely grated
- A handful of parsley, chopped
- Chilli flakes, to taste

## Method

1. Cook the spaghetti in well-salted boiling water until al dente. Save a mug of the cooking water.
2. Melt the butter in a wide pan over a low heat and soften the garlic for 2 minutes without browning.
3. Add the drained pasta, the lemon zest and juice, and a splash of the cooking water. Toss until glossy.
4. Off the heat, stir in the parmesan and parsley. Finish with chilli flakes.
